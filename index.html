🎮 How to Play Monkey Mart

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Start your journey as a monkey with a small empty store
  2. Use the arrow keys or WASD to move your monkey character around
  3. Begin by planting your first banana trees outside your store
  4. Wait for the bananas to grow, then harvest them by walking over them

🛒 Basic Store Operations

  1. Stock your shelves: Walk to empty shelves and press the interaction key to stock them with products
  2. Serve customers: Customers will pick up items and head to the checkout counter
  3. Process payments: Stand behind the cash register and serve customers as they approach
  4. Collect money: Walk over the coins dropped by customers to collect your earnings

📈 Growing Your Business

  1. Open new departments: Use your earnings to unlock new areas like the produce section, bakery, and more
  2. Hire assistants: Purchase monkey helpers to automate stocking, checkout, and other tasks
  3. Upgrade equipment: Improve your cash registers, carts, and production facilities
  4. Expand your farm: Add more crop types and increase your production capacity

💡 Pro Tips

  • Always keep your shelves stocked to maximize customer satisfaction
  • Prioritize hiring assistants for repetitive tasks so you can focus on expansion
  • Watch customer behavior to identify which products are most popular
  • Upgrade your movement speed to serve customers more efficiently
  • Balance between different product types to cater to all customer needs
  • Don't forget to expand your storage capacity as your business grows

🎯 Game Controls

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Left Click: Interact with objects, stock shelves, serve customers
  • Right Click: Access upgrade menus and shop options
  • Mouse Movement: Look around and navigate menus

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys: Move your monkey character
  • Space Bar: Interact with objects and customers
  • Tab: Open/close the upgrade menu
  • Escape: Pause the game or return to main menu
