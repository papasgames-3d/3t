Monkey Mart is a casual, simulation game where players manage and grow their own virtual supermarket. How to Play Monkey Mart

Playing Monkey Mart is as easy as opening your favorite browser or app store on your mobile device. But if you're new to the game, here's a quick guide to get started:

  1. Set Up Your Store: Players begin by unlocking a small store where they can stock basic items such as bananas, oranges, or milk. Customers will purchase these items and pay the player, allowing you to grow your supermarket over time.
  2. Expand Your Business: As you earn more money, you can add new sections to your shop, like deli counters, fruit displays, or even a coffee stand. Expanding your shop not only increases sales but also enhances customer satisfaction.
  3. Train Employees: To manage your store more efficiently, you'll eventually hire employees. They'll help stock shelves, serve customers, and keep your business running smoothly while you focus on bigger upgrades.
  4. Keep Customers Happy: Customer satisfaction is the name of the game. Deliver service quickly, ensure that shelves are always stocked, and keep your store nice and tidy to attract more visitors.
