About Monkey Mart

Welcome to Monkey Mart, the most exciting store management game where you can build and run your own supermarket! Take control of your monkey character and create the most successful store in town.

Key Features of Monkey Mart:

  • Build and customize your own supermarket
  • Manage inventory and serve customers
  • Upgrade your store with new features
  • Train staff and improve efficiency
  • Complete challenges and earn rewards

How to Play Monkey Mart:

  1. Stock your shelves with various products
  2. Serve customers efficiently
  3. Collect money and upgrade your store
  4. Manage your employees
  5. Keep your store clean and organized

Monkey Mart Pro Tips:

  • Keep popular items well-stocked
  • Upgrade your storage capacity early
  • Train your staff regularly
  • Monitor customer satisfaction
  • Plan your store layout efficiently
